What Eve Plants is01

A greener home begins with paying attention.

Plants rarely need more products or more complicated routines. They need someone to notice the light, the soil, the season, and the quiet signs that something is changing.

Eve Plants brings that attention into your home, so your plants can thrive and you can feel confident caring for them.

Ways to work together02

Care that meets you
where you are.

For a single struggling plant, a growing collection, or a whole home that needs a calmer care rhythm.

Online plant-care session

A focused video call, available wherever you are, for plant identification, troubleshooting, placement, and a practical care plan. After the session, you receive clear, structured PDF guidelines based on the visual inspection, plus a follow-up check-in a few weeks later to track progress and adjust care if needed.

02

Plant health visit

A focused session for plants that look unhappy. We identify likely causes and leave you with clear, realistic next steps. After the visit, you receive clear, structured PDF guidelines based on the inspection, with follow-up check-ins included.

↗
03

Plant care guidelines

A home visit to create clear care guidelines for the plants you already have, including suitable soil, a watering routine, light and placement, humidity, and early signals to watch for before health declines. Your tailored guidance is delivered as a clear, structured PDF.

↗
04

Monthly plant care

Regular visits · subscription

Regular home visits for the best wellbeing of your plants, with thoughtful watering, pruning, leaf cleaning, early pest checks, and seasonal care adjustments. After the first visit, you receive a clear, structured PDF report with a care plan. It is updated and shared every month with what has been done and what is planned next.

↗
05

Plant selection for your home

Recommendations based on your actual light, space, pets, habits, and experience, not just what looks good in the shop. You also receive PDF guidelines for the proper care of the selected plants.

A few useful answers

Before we visit.

Do you need to know what plants I have?+

Not at all. Identifying plants and understanding their needs is part of the visit.

Can you help with outdoor plants too?+

Yes. Eve Plants supports both indoor and outdoor plants, with advice shaped around your local conditions and space.

What happens during a monthly visit?+

Plants are checked individually, essential care is carried out, and you receive simple guidance for anything to watch between visits.

Can you help me choose new plants?+

Yes. Recommendations consider light, available space, pets, care habits, and the look you want to create.

Where is Eve Plants available?+

Eve Plants is based in Amsterdam. In-person visits elsewhere in Noord-Holland are available by arrangement, and online plant-care sessions are available wherever you are.
