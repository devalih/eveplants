Dracaena trifasciataEasy

Snake plant

Give a snake plant bright indirect light for its best growth, although it tolerates less. Let the potting mix dry well before watering again, use a draining pot, and keep it away from cold glass in winter.

Zamioculcas zamiifoliaEasy

ZZ plant

Place a ZZ plant in medium to bright indirect light, let the potting mix dry substantially between waterings, and use a compact pot with excellent drainage. Lower light is tolerated, but growth becomes very slow.

Chlorophytum comosumEasy

Spider plant

Grow a spider plant in bright indirect or medium light, water when the upper mix begins to dry, and let excess drain. Give the arching leaves room and root mature plantlets when you want new plants.

Ficus elasticaEasy–moderate

Rubber plant

Give Ficus elastica bright indirect light, water after the upper mix has partly dried, and keep its position warm and stable. Clean the broad leaves and rotate modestly for balanced growth.

SpathiphyllumModerate

Peace lily

Keep a peace lily in medium to bright filtered light and water when the upper mix begins to dry, before repeated severe wilting. Use a draining pot and protect it from cold draughts and radiator air.

Philodendron hederaceumEasy

Heartleaf philodendron

Grow heartleaf philodendron in medium to bright indirect light, keep the mix lightly moist but aerated, and let the upper layer dry before watering again. Trail it or give it a support and prune sparse vines.

Dypsis lutescensModerate

Areca palm

Place an areca palm in bright filtered light, keep the root ball evenly but not permanently wet, and protect the fronds from radiator heat and cold draughts. Give it space and inspect regularly for spider mites.

Howea forsterianaModerate

Kentia palm

Give a Kentia palm medium to bright indirect light, a humus-rich draining mix and steady moisture without saturation. It is tolerant but slow-growing, so avoid repeated repotting and drastic changes.

Nephrolepis exaltataAttentive

Boston fern

Keep a Boston fern in bright indirect light with a consistently lightly moist, well-drained root ball and higher humidity. Protect it from radiator blasts and never let the whole pot dry hard.

Aloe veraEasy in bright light

Aloe vera

Place Aloe vera at a very bright south- or west-facing window, acclimate it to direct sun, and let the mix dry completely before watering deeply. Use a small terracotta pot with several drainage holes.

Crassula ovataEasy in bright light

Jade plant

Keep a jade plant in a very bright window with gradually introduced sun, water only after the mix dries well, and use a heavy draining pot. Prune in active growth to shape the miniature-tree form.

Hoya carnosaEasy–moderate

Wax plant

Give Hoya carnosa bright indirect light with gentle sun, a small pot of loose fast-draining mix, and time to dry partly between waterings. Do not remove old flower spurs, because the plant can bloom from them again.

Ficus lyrataAttentive

Fiddle-leaf fig

Place Ficus lyrata directly beside a bright window, water thoroughly after the upper mix partly dries, and keep light and temperature stable. Clean its large leaves and avoid oversized pots.

Bird of paradise

Give bird of paradise the brightest warm position available, including acclimated direct sun, water after the upper mix partly dries, and allow generous space for the leaves. Indoor flowering requires maturity and exceptional light.

Anthurium andraeanumModerate

Flamingo flower

Keep Anthurium andraeanum in bright filtered light, an airy epiphytic mix and steady warmth. Water after the upper mix begins to dry, drain fully, and protect leaves from radiator air.

Tradescantia zebrina

Give Tradescantia zebrina bright indirect light with some gentle sun, water after the upper mix starts to dry, and pinch or prune regularly. Root fresh node cuttings back into the pot when old vines become bare.

Heptapleurum arboricolaEasy–moderate

Dwarf umbrella tree

Grow dwarf umbrella tree in bright indirect light, water after the upper mix partly dries and prune in active growth to control height. Keep it warm and stable through winter.

Yucca giganteaEasy in bright light

Yucca cane

Put Yucca gigantea in a very bright position with acclimated sun, let much of the mix dry before watering and use a heavy draining pot. Low light plus frequent watering is the main indoor risk.

Schlumbergera truncataEasy–moderate

Christmas cactus

Grow Christmas cactus in bright filtered light and an airy mix, watering after partial drying rather than treating it like a desert cactus. Stable cool nights and long uninterrupted darkness help initiate buds.

PhalaenopsisModerate

Moth orchid

Keep a Phalaenopsis in bright filtered light, water when the exposed roots turn silvery and the bark approaches dry, then drain completely. Use orchid bark and never leave the crown full of water.

AglaonemaEasy–moderate

Chinese evergreen

Keep Aglaonema in medium to bright indirect light, let the upper mix dry before watering and protect it from cold draughts. Colourful cultivars need more light than plain green forms.

Dracaena reflexa var. angustifoliaEasy

Dragon tree

Give a dragon tree medium to bright indirect light, let the upper half of the mix dry before watering, and keep the canes warm. Prune a healthy cane in spring when you want branching.

Monstera deliciosaMedium

Monstera

Place Monstera deliciosa in bright indirect light, water thoroughly after the upper potting mix has partly dried, use a draining chunky mix and give mature stems a support. In Dutch winter, move it closer to light and expect slower drying.

Epipremnum aureumEasy

Pothos

Grow Pothos in medium to bright indirect light, water after the upper mix dries, and use a pot with drainage. Trim sparse vines above a node and propagate node-bearing cuttings. Move it closer to a window in Dutch winter and reduce watering as growth slows.

Pilea peperomioidesEasy

Pilea

Keep Pilea in bright indirect light, water after the upper mix dries and rotate it regularly for even growth. Separate rooted pups only when they have their own small root system, then keep the new plants in stable bright light.

Calathea

Give Calathea bright filtered light, an airy mix that stays lightly moist without becoming stagnant, and a warm position away from radiators and cold glass. Treat brown edges as a clue to investigate, not proof that tap water is the only cause.

Peperomia obtusifoliaEasy

Baby rubber plant

Keep Peperomia obtusifolia in bright indirect light, let a good portion of the mix dry before watering, and use a small airy pot. Its fleshy leaves store water, so frequent watering is unnecessary.

Pachira aquaticaEasy

Money tree

Place a money tree in bright indirect light, water thoroughly once the upper part of the mix has dried, and always let excess water drain away. Keep its braided trunk warm and away from cold glass, and rotate it occasionally for even growth.
